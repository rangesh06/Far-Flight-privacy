FAR FLIGHT: Space Odyssey — Privacy Policy

Published by Spawn Bridge · Effective November 20, 2026

In short: FAR FLIGHT: Space Odyssey (“the game”) does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or online features. Your progress is saved only on your headset, and Spawn Bridge never receives it.

Who we are

Spawn Bridge (“we”, “us”) develops and publishes the game for Meta Quest headsets through the Meta Horizon Store. This policy explains what information the game uses and how. If you have questions, contact us at rangeshus@gmail.com.

Information saved on your headset

To keep your progress between sessions, the game saves a small file in its private storage on your headset. It contains:

  • your game settings;
  • campaign progress, including which events you have completed and how many times you have started each one;
  • story discoveries you have unlocked;
  • your best time on each course and the route you flew.

This file is used only to run the game for you. It does not contain your name, email address or any other information that identifies you, and the game never sends it to us or anyone else.

Hand and head tracking

You play the game with your hands. The headset’s hand tracking and head tracking tell the game where your hands and head are, so you can grip the flight stick and throttle, press the cockpit controls and look around the cockpit. The game uses this information in real time, on the headset, for those purposes only. It does not record, save or transmit your hand, head or body movements.

Information we do not collect

The game does not collect or transmit:

  • names, usernames, email addresses or other contact details;
  • your Meta account information or friends list;
  • device identifiers, IP addresses or location;
  • microphone audio, camera or passthrough images, or eye or face tracking data;
  • analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or advertising data.

The game shows no ads and contains no in-app purchases.

Meta

The game is sold and delivered through the Meta Horizon Store and runs on Meta Horizon OS. Meta processes information about your purchase, your account and your device under its own privacy policies, which you can find on meta.com. If you turn on cloud backup in your headset settings, Meta may also back up the game’s save file to your Meta account. We do not have access to that information.

Deleting your data

Because your data stays on your headset, you control it:

  • Uninstall the game to delete its save file from your headset.
  • Delete any cloud backup of the game in your headset’s backup settings or your Meta account settings.
  • Email us at rangeshus@gmail.com with any deletion request or question. We hold no personal data about players, but we will confirm this and help you remove the game’s data from your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Please follow Meta’s age requirements for your headset.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the game collects, for example by adding online features, we will update this policy before releasing that update and change the effective date above.
